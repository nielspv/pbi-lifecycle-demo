# Definition of Ready

A PBI is Ready when all of the following are met:

1. **Clear title and summary** – understandable without extra context.
2. **User story** – role, goal and benefit are stated.
3. **Business value** – why this matters is explicit.
4. **Acceptance criteria** – at least three, testable, preferably Given/When/Then.
5. **Scope boundaries** – what is out of scope is stated.
6. **Dependencies and risks** – identified or explicitly "none".
7. **Estimate** – sized by the team (not "Unknown").
8. **Non-functional needs** – security, performance, accessibility considered.
